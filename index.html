Where the dust-front numbers come from
  • measured Front speed 25 m/s: the January 2022 storm front at Jezero, from Perseverance data (Sánchez-Lavega et al. 2025). Fronts in the 2018 storm ran 5 to 40 m/s.
  • assumed A straight front moving east, a 90 s confirmation delay, and the station layout. No such network exists on Mars today.
  • model Warning time = distance to the first sensor the front crosses ÷ front speed − confirmation delay. A second team upwind counts as a sensor.
  • scenario You choose when the front arrives. It is not a forecast.

Full evidence and links: docs/RESEARCH.md. Planet-scale spread: open the Globe.
